About SNEPCO GMT

We develop people who move organisations forward.

A Nigerian capacity development and professional services company helping individuals, teams and organisations perform with greater confidence.

Who we are

Practical expertise. Human understanding. Meaningful progress.

SNEPCO GMT is a Port Harcourt-based company focused on the people side of performance.

We work with organisations and professionals through human capacity development, professional training, hiring and consultancy. Our approach is rooted in a simple belief: when people have the right skills, support and opportunities, better outcomes follow.

From a single learning programme to a wider talent or organisational challenge, we bring clear thinking, responsive delivery and a commitment to results that matter.
